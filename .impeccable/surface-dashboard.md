# Public marine dashboard
Mode: Operate. Audience: general public, confirmed 28 October 2026.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A bright Thai marine atlas that puts the explorable map before technical summaries. Citizens select a coast, point and day, then read physical values and transparent activity constraints.

OWN-WORLD: Saturated ocean blue masthead, white working panels, turquoise controls, apricot temperature accents and violet rain accents. Locally hosted Thai typography; no decorative photographic assets.

STORY: Understand conditions, compare six forecast days, inspect the reasons for screening, and consult the official warning source.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Compact navigation, blue introduction, four linked physical measurements and a large map with an adjacent point inspector. Layer buttons and timeline are always adjacent to the map. Detailed science follows the working surface.

FORM: User-directed bright interactive atlas. Context and concept-seed launchers attempted but unavailable because the engine is absent and its cache cannot be written. No random assignment or approved comp is claimed. Code-led implementation uses the user's confirmed audience and explicit visual brief.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
